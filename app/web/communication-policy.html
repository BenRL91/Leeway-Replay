LeewayPB communication policy · October 9, 2026

Your attention, your choice.

Everything email is opt-in only. Creating an account, entering a contact email, joining an app, making a booking or payment, submitting footage, or receiving a breakdown does not give us permission to email you. We do not assume a transactional-email exemption.

Any future email choice must be separate, unselected by default, and explain its purpose and app. In-app notification and push choices are separate from email consent. Reading a notification does not grant consent.

Leaving should be easy

Every email must include a visually prominent direct unsubscribe. No survey, explanation, forced sign-in, or retention flow. Unsubscribing stops queued sends and retries. We will not automatically resubscribe you.

A link opens a page with one direct unsubscribe action and an immediate success confirmation. Opening the link alone does not change your preference, so email link scanners cannot unsubscribe you by accident. Supported email clients will also have one-click unsubscribe.

Replay today

Replay update emails and push notifications are disabled. No Replay email opt-in or unsubscribe service is available yet. The in-app notification inbox is active when its shared service is available; its saved preference controls the unread badge.

Sign-in and payment providers may generate their own messages. Their configuration and compliance require a separate audit before customer beta. This policy update does not change those providers.

We will not pressure you with repeated prompts to turn communications on.
